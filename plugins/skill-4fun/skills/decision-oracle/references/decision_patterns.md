# Decision Patterns

## Answers

- Yes, but make it smaller.
- No, unless the cost drops.
- Wait one day and ask again with better information.
- Choose the option that creates fewer future obligations.
- Choose the scarier option only if it matches your stated values.
- Split the decision into a reversible test.

## Caveats

- The omen dislikes vague calendars.
- The sign changes if money, safety, or consent changes.
- The answer is about the next step, not the whole life path.
- The oracle refuses to replace expert advice.
