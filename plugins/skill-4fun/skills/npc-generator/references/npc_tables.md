# NPC Tables

## Roles

- Innkeeper, courier, archivist, mechanic, temple guard, street doctor, smuggler, apprentice mage.
- Station quartermaster, asteroid miner, AI ethicist, salvage pilot, diplomatic aide.
- Barista, office manager, night guard, landlord, retired detective, local journalist.

## Motives

- Protect someone, escape debt, prove competence, bury a mistake, recover an heirloom, expose a lie.

## Mannerisms

- Answers questions with questions.
- Polishes a tool while thinking.
- Never uses names twice.
- Laughs before saying bad news.
- Carries too many keys.

## Secrets

- Knows the villain personally.
- Is hiding a map in plain sight.
- Has already solved half the mystery.
- Works for two factions and trusts neither.
- Is cursed, but only on market days.
