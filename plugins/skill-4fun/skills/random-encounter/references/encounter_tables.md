# Encounter Tables

## Settings

- Dungeon: locked shrine, flooded corridor, whispering mural, rival delvers.
- Wilderness: broken bridge, hungry guide, false trail, storm sign.
- City: parade blockage, rooftop chase, market argument, missing courier.
- Space: derelict beacon, pressure leak, suspicious cargo, silent shuttle.
- Office: surprise audit, vanished laptop, broken coffee machine, locked conference room.

## Complications

- Someone is lying for a sympathetic reason.
- The obvious threat is a distraction.
- Time pressure grows every few minutes.
- The reward belongs to someone else.
- Solving the problem creates a new obligation.
