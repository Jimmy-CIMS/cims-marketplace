# Spell Tables

## Schools

- Lantern, Mirror, Thorn, Clock, Salt, Archive, Door, Weather, Static, Echo.

## Effects

- Reveal a hidden path for one minute.
- Borrow the sound of a place.
- Turn a promise into visible thread.
- Delay a falling object by three heartbeats.
- Make a locked thing remember being open.
- Translate the mood of a room into color.

## Costs

- You forget a trivial word for one hour.
- The spell leaves a visible mark until sunset.
- It only works if spoken honestly.
- It attracts attention from something bored.
- It consumes a small written memory.

## Mishaps

- The effect works too politely.
- The target hears your internal monologue for a sentence.
- Nearby shadows point toward you.
- The spell repeats itself at half strength tomorrow.
