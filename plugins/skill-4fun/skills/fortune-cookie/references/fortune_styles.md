# Fortune Styles

- Classic: hopeful, compact, slightly mysterious.
- Gentle: calming, emotionally supportive, practical.
- Absurd: surreal but still readable.
- Zen: quiet, paradoxical, nature-inflected.
- Workday: about focus, meetings, deadlines, craft, and momentum.
- Romantic: affectionate without manipulation or certainty.
- Ominous: theatrical and playful, not threatening.

## Seeds

- A closed door may be a wall pretending to be advice.
- The small delay protects the larger arrival.
- Ask the question again after tea.
- Your next useful answer will be hiding in a boring place.
- A lucky mistake is still asking to be noticed.
