# Major Arcana Quick Meanings

- The Fool: beginnings, risk, openness. Reversed: hesitation, recklessness, naivete.
- The Magician: agency, tools, focus. Reversed: misdirection, scattered effort, unused skill.
- The High Priestess: intuition, silence, hidden knowledge. Reversed: confusion, secrecy, ignored instincts.
- The Empress: growth, care, abundance. Reversed: depletion, overgiving, creative block.
- The Emperor: structure, authority, boundaries. Reversed: rigidity, control, weak foundations.
- The Hierophant: tradition, teaching, belonging. Reversed: conformity, stale rules, private belief.
- The Lovers: choice, alignment, values. Reversed: imbalance, avoidance, mixed motives.
- The Chariot: discipline, momentum, resolve. Reversed: drift, force, divided will.
- Strength: patience, courage, compassion. Reversed: self-doubt, pressure, raw emotion.
- The Hermit: solitude, study, inner light. Reversed: isolation, avoidance, lost perspective.
- Wheel of Fortune: change, cycles, luck. Reversed: resistance, delay, unstable timing.
- Justice: fairness, truth, consequences. Reversed: bias, evasion, imbalance.
- The Hanged Man: pause, surrender, new angle. Reversed: stagnation, martyrdom, refusal.
- Death: ending, release, transformation. Reversed: clinging, fear of change, unfinished closure.
- Temperance: balance, blending, patience. Reversed: excess, friction, poor pacing.
- The Devil: attachment, appetite, shadow. Reversed: release, awareness, loosening bonds.
- The Tower: disruption, revelation, collapse. Reversed: delayed truth, fear, rebuilding.
- The Star: hope, renewal, trust. Reversed: discouragement, distance, dimmed faith.
- The Moon: uncertainty, dreams, illusion. Reversed: clarity, anxiety, emerging truth.
- The Sun: vitality, joy, success. Reversed: muted joy, ego, temporary clouds.
- Judgement: reckoning, awakening, calling. Reversed: self-judgment, delay, refusal.
- The World: completion, integration, arrival. Reversed: loose ends, unfinished lessons, near-completion.
